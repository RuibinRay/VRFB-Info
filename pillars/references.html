How this site is sourced

Every bracketed number on the site links to an entry below. Standard electrode potentials, concentrations, temperature limits and efficiencies come from peer-reviewed reviews and primary papers; cost figures come from U.S. national-laboratory and industry assessments and are presented as ranges or editable assumptions, never as quotes. Where a value is a rule of thumb or a model simplification, the page says so next to the number.

Four kinds of number appear on the site, and they are not equally solid:

Measured data: laboratory or field measurements from the cited papersModel results: calculated by this site's documented, tested modelsIllustrative defaults: editable starting values inside published rangesManufacturer claims: stated by suppliers, not independently verified here

Links point to DOIs or publisher pages where available. If you find an error or a better source, open an issue on the repository.

Notes on specific numbers

  • E° values. −0.255 V (V³⁺/V²⁺) and +1.004 V (VO₂⁺/VO²⁺) vs SHE are the widely tabulated standard potentials; the literature also quotes −0.26 V and +1.00 V. The cell standard potential is therefore 1.26 V.
  • Open-circuit voltage. ≈ 1.40 V at 50 % SOC is the measured value discussed by Knehr and Kumbur [15]; the simple Nernst form predicts about 1.34 V in 5 M acid.
  • Energy density. 20–35 Wh/L refers to the total of both electrolytes; some sources quote per-side values twice as high.
  • Costs. The calculator defaults (stacks $450/kW, power conversion $100/kW, V₂O₅ $18/kg, balance of system $60/kWh + $150/kW, LFP $250 per nameplate kWh) are illustrative values within the ranges in [5], [6], [14], [19] and [23], not vendor data. Battery prices, LFP prices especially, change quickly: replace them with current figures.
  • Lifetimes. Cycle-life and calendar-life figures above about 10 000 cycles or 20 years are design targets or manufacturer claims for both technologies; long-term field data at grid scale is still limited [5], [19].
  • Efficiency. 65–80 % AC round trip for vanadium flow systems and 85–90 % for LFP are ranges from [5]; a specific product may sit outside them.
  • Flow and pumping. Flow factors of 5–10 and pump shares of 2–5 % are typical values from [14] and [20]; the optimum depends on the stack design and the state of charge.
  • Models. The calculation modules behind every tool are in js/sim/ of the repository and are covered by automated tests (npm test), including energy balance, charge balance of the reactions and hand-checked LCOS cases.
  • Project figures. Dalian phase 1 (100 MW / 400 MWh, 2022) per DICP/Rongke Power announcements [7]; Hokkaido figures per Sumitomo Electric [8].
